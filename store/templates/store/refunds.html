Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: Today

1. 30-Day Return Window

We want you to love your new T-shirts! You have 30 days after receiving your item to request a return. To be eligible, your item must be in the same condition that you received it, unworn or unused, with tags.

2. The Refund Process

Once we receive and inspect your return, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund. If approved, you will be automatically refunded on your original payment method via Razorpay within 5-7 business days.

3. Damaged or Wrong Items

Please inspect your order upon reception. Contact us immediately if the item is defective, damaged, or if you received the wrong item, so that we can evaluate the issue and make it right immediately.
